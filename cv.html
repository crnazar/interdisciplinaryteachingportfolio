Curriculum Vitae

Christina Restrepo Nazar, Ph.D.

Teacher education, STEM education, and digital humanities — grounded in how devoted engagement with learning transforms both educators and students.

crn@ucf.edu christinarestreponazar.com Orlando, FL

Nazar's research spans teacher education, STEM education, digital learning, and artificial intelligence. Her work examines how educators can use technology, hands-on experiences, and student-created digital materials not as substitutes for human connection, but as vessels for deeper understanding — and, in her Texts & Technology doctoral work, how digital systems shape scientific and technical communication across learning environments. This is an abbreviated CV; a full record is available on request.
