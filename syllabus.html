A note on this course's development

This seminar grew out of my course Encountering the World. An earlier version gathered the whole project only at the end of term — polished, but the interpretive process stayed hidden. This version scaffolds the work week by week so the analytical argument is finalized before any machine account is generated. The syllabus is a living document, always subject to change.

Purpose of the course

Artificial intelligence now explains the world to us. It answers our students. It drafts our writing. It narrates science as if from nowhere. This course treats AI as a cultural and epistemological phenomenon, not a neutral tool. We learn to read AI outputs as situated and partial. We learn to name what a fluent, confident account leaves out.

The work is hands-on. You will build and critique interactive artifacts rather than hand your thinking to a machine. You will do real observation. You will put a lived encounter against a machine account of the same thing, and study the gap. The seam between the two is where the course lives. The seminar is open to graduate students from education, the sciences, the humanities, and design. Each of you will anchor the work in a phenomenon and a field you care about.

Course philosophy

I think about AI the way I think about science. It is a culture. It has ways of knowing, central assumptions, and a voice that sounds like no one and everyone at once. A fluent AI account can feel like the truth from nowhere. It is not. It is made, and the making shows.

One job of this course is to help you see the seam. Facts, definitions, and procedures cross easily into a machine account. The lived, the embodied, the situated, and the attended do not. This is the border I care about — the border my own work calls what the machine cannot cross. So we will not ask AI to do our thinking. We will use it as a foil.

Student learning outcomes

Upon completion, students will be able to:

  • Analyze AI outputs as situated cultural and epistemological artifacts rather than neutral sources.
  • Apply critical frameworks — borderlands thinking, Actor-Network Theory, and critical AI studies — to a technology in active use.
  • Evaluate the affordances and limits of AI for teaching and knowledge-making in a specific domain.
  • Create an interactive digital artifact that stages a critical encounter with AI.
  • Document AI use transparently and appraise the biases and limits encountered.
  • Adapt a critical method to their own disciplinary or teaching context.

Artificial intelligence policy

Using AI is part of the work in this course. The failure is not using it. The failure is hiding it. I want your process visible so I can teach into it.

  • Encouraged. Use AI to generate accounts you will critique, to brainstorm, to get feedback on a draft, and to build your artifacts. This is the material of the course, not a shortcut around it.
  • Required for major assignments. Disclose your use. Include the substantive prompts you ran. Reflect on what the tool did well, where it failed, and at least one bias or limit you saw.
  • Not allowed. Do not submit AI-written analysis as your own thinking. The analysis is the skill I am assessing. You may use AI to react to your ideas; you may not let it replace them.
  • Responsibility & privacy. You are responsible for everything you submit, including any error the tool introduced. Verify factual claims. Do not put other people's private or sensitive information into an AI tool.

Additional policies

Late work. Reading responses are accepted up to 48 hours late for half credit. Larger assignments lose 3% per day for up to seven days. Everyone gets one automatic 72-hour extension on a single major assignment, no questions asked, if requested before the deadline. Life happens — contact me early and we will find a way through it.

Attendance & participation. This is a once-a-week seminar, so your presence matters. Come prepared and take part. If you must miss for illness or an emergency, email me in advance.

Gender expression and identity. This course affirms people of all gender expressions and identities. I will gladly honor your request to be addressed by the name and pronouns you use. Please tell me at any time so I can update my records.
