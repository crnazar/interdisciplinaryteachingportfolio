What the machine cannot cross.

I teach students to slow down at the interface — to describe the world carefully with their own senses before handing anything to a machine. My courses treat artificial intelligence as a cultural artifact to be read critically, and use it as a foil for the lived, embodied, situated knowing that no fluent account can reproduce.

Read my teaching statement See the seminar

The seam

Some knowledge crosses easily into a machine account — facts, definitions, procedures. The lived, the embodied, the situated, and the attended do not. That border is where my teaching lives.
