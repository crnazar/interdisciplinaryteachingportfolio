On process, and on AI & pedagogy

A space for my reflection on building this portfolio and on how the semester reshaped how I think about the relationship of AI and pedagogy.

Draft space — I'll write my reflection here. The scaffolding below mirrors the assignment: a working portfolio link, at least three readings from across the semester, and an honest account of how my position on AI has (or hasn't) shifted.

On the process

What was it like to plan and build a portfolio through an agentic workflow — using planning mode, giving style and structural guidance, and iterating? What surprised me? What did I control well, and where did I have to let go?

Readings that shaped my thinking

Cite at least three readings from across the semester and connect them to how I now think about digital-humanities pedagogy broadly. (Candidates from the seminar: Haraway, Situated Knowledges; Anzaldúa, Borderlands; Benjamin, Race After Technology; Crawford, Atlas of AI; Noble, Algorithms of Oppression; Onuoha, The Library of Missing Datasets; Bender et al., Stochastic Parrots; Kimmerer, Braiding Sweetgrass; Salter et al., Critical Making in the Age of AI; Philip, Irani & Dourish, Postcolonial Computing.)

Where I've landed on AI & pedagogy

Looking back on the whole semester, how has my position on AI in the classroom — and in my own work — shifted, or stayed the same? What will I carry into my teaching?
